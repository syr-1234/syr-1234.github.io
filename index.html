Making the silenced visible.

I’m a Digital Media student at the University of Leeds. My work turns what gets filtered out, by algorithms, by data, by the way stories are told, into websites, visualisations, images and narratives people can see and engage with.

Critical thinking, made into things.

I’m interested in how platforms, data and narrative systems decide what becomes visible. I study those systems, then build work that makes their effects perceptible: from a research archive on suppressed women’s health content to a visualisation of who gets paid for a stream.
