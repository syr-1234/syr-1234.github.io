Why we chose this theme - The Silent Flock

We chose this topic because we saw that women's health content was suppressed by platforms and algorithms, which is really happening around us. Many of our female friends and family have experienced confusion or frustration when trying to find reliable women's health information online. At the same time, we've noticed that posts about women's bodies and health conditions on social media platforms are often more likely to be restricted, hidden, or even deleted. This is not just a minor issue. It affects how women understand their health.

Our website is named Silent "Sheep". There are several reasons for this name. Sheep are generally considered obedient and easy to control. In our case, “group” refers to posts and voices talking about women’s health. They are both controlled and excluded by algorithms. The word “silenced” means that these voices are hidden and blocked by the platform’s rules. Additionally, the shape of the ovaries looks very similar to the shape of a sheep's head. This connection makes sense to us. The image we used shows a herd of sheep breaking free, which represents women trying to speak out despite being constrained by algorithms

To be clear, we are not comparing women to animals. This is just a metaphor. Sheep within the flock are often guided and controlled by outside forces—just as posts about women’s health are pushed aside or hidden by platform algorithms. We use this image and this metaphor only to better help explain how algorithmic control works, and not to disrespect anyone.

Through this website we hope to raise awareness of how women's health information is censored online. Our target audience is women aged 18 to 25 years. Not only are this age group active online, but they are also at higher risk of developing conditions like PCOS. When platforms block or restrict health-related content, young women may miss out on important information or have misunderstandings. We hope The Silent Flock can be a small space for open discussion and real stories.
