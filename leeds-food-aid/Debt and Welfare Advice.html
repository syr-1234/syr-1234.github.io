Money – Debt and Budgeting

The Leeds Money Information Centre

The Leeds Money Information Centre website and publications provide details of all the organisations and charities across Leeds providing free and independent debt advice, including the Citizens Advice Bureau, StepChange and many more (see below). Depending on your preference you can either contact them by phone, online or even face-to-face. Lots of centres and organisations across the city are branded as MICs, such as the credit union branches, debt advice agencies, Council One Stop Centres and libraries.

Ebor Gardens Advice Centre (EGAC) is a local charity, based in Burmantofts, giving free, confidential and impartial advice in relation to debt (facing eviction, bailiffs at the door, door step collectors, County Court Judgements or just not managing to pay debts). They can represent people, advise on their rights and provide a casework service where they negotiate a realistic repayment program. They also run the Money Buddies Scheme.

Money Buddies provide one-to one help. They can help with following:- debt advice, working on a budget, inputting information on to a computer, printing letters off for creditors, maximising income, filling out forms to apply for grants and saving money by finding the cheapest gas / electricity supplier.

Loan Sharks

Loan Sharks are illegal money lenders who often charge very high interest rates. If someone has borrowed money from one, or to check if a company is authorised to lend money, a report can be made anonymously by contacting the Illegal Money Lending Team.

To report a loan shark by telephone call the 24/7 confidential hotline on 0300 555 2222

To report a loan shark by Text; Text ‘loan shark + your message’ to 60003

The National Debtline

The National Debtline provides online and telephone advice and information for individuals experiencing debt and financial difficulties. 40+ factsheets are available online, along with budgeting tools and sample letters.

Debt advice is also available by using the online webchat or emailing service, or by calling the helpline 0808 808 4000 – Mon-Fri 9am-8pm, Sat: 9.30am-1pm
