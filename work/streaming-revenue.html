Plays for a minimum wage

How many times does a song have to be played for a musician to make a living? I imported a public dataset on musicians’ digital royalties into a MySQL database, read it with PHP and charted the answer, outlet by outlet, with Chart.js.

Course

Year
2025
My role
Individual project
Tools
PHP, MySQL (cPanel), Chart.js
Data
Musician digital royalties (2013), InformationIsBeautiful.net

Why this data

I chose musicians’ digital royalties because the numbers themselves make the strongest case: per-play earnings are so low that it takes millions of plays to earn a living. Schrock (2016) describes civic hacking as data activism: requesting, digesting, modelling and contesting data. Turning a raw table into something anyone can read is the digesting and modelling part.

How it works

I created a MySQL database in cPanel and imported the dataset. Two PHP pages read from it:

  • Data page: lists each outlet with artist type, format, retail price, and the plays per month needed to earn a minimum wage.
  • Chart page: sends the same plays-per-month figures to Chart.js as a bar chart, one bar per outlet.

What it shows

Once charted, the gap is hard to ignore. On Last.fm, a musician needs more than 15 million plays a month to earn a minimum wage.

Looking back

As media students we can do more than build websites. Obtaining, analysing and questioning data are ways to take on social responsibility, and code can bring the hidden workings of abstract systems to light.
