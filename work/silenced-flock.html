The Silenced Flock

How do platform algorithms, community guidelines and commercial moderation keep women’s health content out of sight? I led a team to find out, then built a public archive of the cases we documented.

The question

Content about menstruation, contraception, reproductive health and other women’s health topics is often down-ranked, labelled or removed. We wanted to know how often this happens, who it affects, and what it does to people’s access to public health information.
