Scraping a Wikipedia article

My first web scraper, written in Python in Google Colab, collected the structure of Wikipedia’s “Artificial Intelligence” article. The headings, sub-entries and order of references turned out to be decisions negotiated among editors: what a scraper returns is knowledge that has already been filtered.
