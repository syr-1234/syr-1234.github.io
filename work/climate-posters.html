Global warming poster series

Global warming raises sea levels, makes extreme weather more frequent and threatens economies, health and living conditions, yet its scale makes it hard to grasp. For a brief on design for social change, I made a three-part series that translates the science into a visual language people can feel.

The brief

The brief asked for design for social change. I chose global warming because it is one of the most pressing issues we face. The goal was to resonate with the audience, turn scientific information into something perceptible, and make people aware that every step they take can make a difference.

The series

The three posters work as a sequence, from attention to understanding to action.

  • Cover: a globe split between a burning half and a frozen half, under the title Global Warming: A Call to Action.
  • Infographic: charts and icons over a world map set out causes such as industry, agriculture and fossil fuels, and effects such as extreme weather, rising sea levels and damage to ecosystems, alongside toxic waste and water, air and soil pollution.
  • Act now: what individuals can do, from cutting fossil fuel use to recycling, and what governments should do, from clean energy to international cooperation.
